AstraForge 0.4.4 — for everyone: the studio learns from your ratings, Unity export, the "2D" tab.

• Self-learning: a production journal (what was made, with which settings, and your verdict) turns into recipes per asset class — see "Review → Journal and recipes".
• Unity 6 export: a new "unity" profile and the com.astraforge.unity importer bring the model, rig and animations into a Unity project ready to use. For Claude Code: MCP for Unity support and the astraforge-integrator agent that takes an asset from the studio to a working scene in Unity or Godot.
• "2D" tab: images, character views, body parts, animation frames and storyboards for games. Note: out of the box "2D" runs on SDXL. The installer does not yet set up FLUX.2 klein, Qwen-Image or the ComfyUI-GGUF node they need, so the tab tells you what is missing. Drawing from a reference (FLUX.2) arrives once those models are in the installer. Picking models during setup and generating through GPT with a ChatGPT subscription come in the next version.
• Game generators: destructible houses, zombies, turrets, 16 directions for pixel sprites.
